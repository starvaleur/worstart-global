# Confirmed Content Needed Before Launch

The homepage is intentionally ready to accept the following company-owned details when they are confirmed:

- Legal company name and registration wording
- Official business email address
- Office or registered address, if it should be public
- Phone number, if it should be public
- Social profile URLs
- Privacy policy and terms URLs
- Final brand mark and approved favicon
- Preferred language versions and translation review

Until supplied and approved, these details should remain clearly marked as placeholders. Do not replace them with invented values.
